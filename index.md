# Qiblah Pointer Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 October 2026

Qiblah Pointer is a map-based application designed to help users identify the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah. This Privacy Policy explains how the app uses location information and third-party services.

## 1. Information and Location

Qiblah Pointer can use your device's location, with your permission, to display your current position on the map and calculate the direction to the Kaaba.

You can also search for a place manually. Location permission can be controlled through your device's app settings. If you deny location permission, the current-location feature may not work, but manual place search may still be available.

## 2. Map and Search Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

- **OpenStreetMap:** Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers to display the map. Requests to those servers may expose technical information such as your device's IP address and the map area being requested. See the [OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Photon:** Place searches are sent to the Photon geocoding service, which uses OpenStreetMap data. The search text you enter is sent to the service to find matching places. See the [Photon project](https://github.com/komoot/photon).

These services operate under their own policies and practices.

## 3. How Location Is Used

The app uses location information to show your position and calculate a line towards the Kaaba. Based on the app's current implementation, your device's GPS coordinates are not sent to Photon as part of a place-search request.

Map and search requests do involve communication with the relevant service providers, as described above.

## 4. Data Collection and Sharing

The app is not designed to require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, or other personal profile details.

The app developer does not intentionally collect or sell personal information through the app. However, third-party map and search services may receive technical information when your device connects to them. Please consult their policies for details.

## 5. Advertising and Analytics

This policy does not imply that third-party services never process technical information. If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other services are added to the app in the future, this policy will be updated as appropriate.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Qiblah Pointer is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

## 7. Security and Data Retention

The app developer does not operate a separate account system or personal-data database for this app. Technical information handled by third-party service providers is subject to their own retention and security practices.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.

## 9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer through the contact details provided on the Qiblah Pointer Google Play Store listing.
